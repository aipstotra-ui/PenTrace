How it works

Two signals, fused into one clean line of text.

PenTrace never guesses whether your pen is touching the page. It watches, and it checks against the ink itself.

01 / TRACK

Follow the pen

The camera locks onto your hand and pen tip, following every motion in real time as you write.

02 / CONFIRM

Check the ink

A stroke is only committed when real ink appears on the page beneath it — so hovering and lifting never leave a mark.

03 / DIGITIZE

Read it back

Clean, evenly-lit strokes are handed to AI recognition — far better input than any photo — and become editable text.

The idea inside

Motion says where. Ink says when.

Camera pen-tracking alone can't tell writing from hovering — the flaw that sank every prior attempt. PenTrace pairs it with the ink on the page, so the two confirm each other.

01

Motion layer

Tracks the pen tip frame by frame. Fast, but can't feel the paper.

02

Ink layer

Reads new ink appearing on a flattened, top-down view of your page. The ground truth.

03

Fusion

A stroke commits only where motion and fresh ink agree. Nothing you write is lost; nothing you didn't is invented.
